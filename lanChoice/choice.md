## Easy to use / low learning curve
Go was designed to be small and simple — minimal syntax, one clear way to do things, and a strong standard library. 
In Go's own 2025 developer survey, 91% of respondents reported satisfaction, and developers specifically credit Go's simplicity as why it works well even for less experienced teams.

## Widely used
Per the 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey (49,000+ respondents, 177 countries), Go is used by 16.4% of developers worldwide — ahead of Rust and Kotlin — with usage still growing year over year.

## Built for concurrency
Go has concurrency (goroutines, channels) built directly into the language, rather than bolted on as a library — making it well-suited for backend/API work without needing complex external tooling.

> Sources: https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/technology
